# Version differences

Notes for possible future "play modes" (other versions' rules). Facts are sourced (links at the bottom);
anything marked **to verify** still needs checking.

## Rosters and modes

| Version | Added characters | Modes |
|---|---|---|
| **Arcade, CPS2 (1998)** | 28: the Alpha 2 cast plus Cammy, E. Honda, Blanka, Balrog (hidden), Vega, R. Mika, Karin, Cody, Juli and Juni (hidden) | Arcade. A-ism, X-ism, V-ism. Hidden Dramatic Battle (two players against one CPU). |
| **PlayStation (1998)** | Juli, Juni and Balrog selectable; Dee Jay, Fei Long, T. Hawk new; Guile, Evil Ryu, Shin Akuma unlockable | World Tour (first appearance), Dramatic Battle with full campaigns only for Ryu & Ken and Juli & Juni, Survival, Final Battle. Animation frames cut for memory. |
| **Saturn, Japan (1999), needs the 4 MB RAM cart** | Guile, Evil Ryu, Shin Akuma selectable from the start; Final Bison by code | World Tour and Survival as PS1. **Dramatic Battle with 2-player mode, Reverse Dramatic Battle, and battles against the full roster.** Extra frames and sprites over the PS1. |
| **Dreamcast (1999)** | as Saturn | Revised World Tour, Saikyo Dojo, I-ism, online score display; Matching Service release adds online VS. |
| **Upper, NAOMI arcade (2001)** | the 6 console characters (Dee Jay, Fei Long, T. Hawk, Guile, Evil Ryu, Shin Akuma) | Balance changes, including removal of the crouch-cancel glitch behind V-ism infinites. |
| **Upper, GBA (2002)** | Yun, Maki, Eagle | Reduced stages and music. |
| **MAX, PSP (2006): the one recompiled** | Ingrid; 38–39 selectable in all | Arcade, Training, Survival, World Tour (with I-ism), Edit, Dramatic Battle with any pairing, Reverse Dramatic, Variable Battle (tag, 2-on-1), 100 Kumite, Drama Survival. Wireless VS, Team, Tournament, League and up to 3-player Dramatic/Variable. No true offline VS. |
| **Capcom Fighting Collection 2 (2025): Upper** | August 2025 update adds Ingrid, Maki, Yun, Eagle via EX Settings → Additional Characters (offline only) | EX Settings → Mode Select adds MAZI, SAIKYO and CLASSIC fighting styles on the select screen. Offline toggles for secret characters, one-button supers, difficulty. Rollback online, training with input display and hitboxes. |

## Arcade notes

- Regional differences: Japan and Asia call it Zero 3 and use "Z-ISM", elsewhere "A-ISM"; select-screen
  text, title, announcer and the pre-fight segue change to match.
- The regulation setting (on by default outside Japan) removes Zangief's pre-stage blood and censors
  Rose's ending. The PSP version shows the regulation-off versions.
- The changes between the arcade revisions (980629, 980701, 980727, 980904) are **to verify**.
